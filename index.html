Who am I?

    I am Isabella! I've recently graduated from the University of Edinburgh with a degree in maths, but what I really want to devote myself to is fighting the climate crisis. While at uni I did lots of work as part of Dirty Weekenders, Permaculture Gardening Society, Climate Community, and Slurp. Though I'm no longer part of Slurp, I am still running the other three groups! I am now regularly volunteering with Changeworks and I've just started running mending workshops at the university! I've also got a little mini business cutting people's hair!
